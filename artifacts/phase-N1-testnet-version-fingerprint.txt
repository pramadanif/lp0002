## Method
A LEZ ProgramId is the risc0 ImageID of the program ELF. LEZ ships prebuilt program
binaries in artifacts/ at each tag. Computing the ImageID of those committed binaries
and comparing against the live testnet's getProgramIds identifies the deployed version.
ImageIDs computed host-side with risc0-zkvm 3.0.5 compute_image_id().
